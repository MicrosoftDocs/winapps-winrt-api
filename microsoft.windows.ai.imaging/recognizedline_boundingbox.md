## -description

Gets the position and size of the quadrilateral polygon that surrounds the [RecognizedLine](recognizedline.md).

## -property-value

The position and size of the quadrilateral polygon that surrounds the line.

## -remarks

[`TopLeft`](recognizedtextboundingbox.md) is relative to the rotation of the line.
