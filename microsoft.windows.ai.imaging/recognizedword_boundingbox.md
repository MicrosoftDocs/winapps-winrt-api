<!--

## -description

Gets the position and size of the quadrilateral polygon that surrounds the [RecognizedWord](recognizedword.md).

## -property-value

The position and size of the quadrilateral polygon that surrounds the recognized word.

## -remarks

[`TopLeft`](recognizedtextboundingbox.md) is relative to the rotation of the word.
