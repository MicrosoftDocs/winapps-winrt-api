# Microsoft.Windows.AI.Text.TextRewriteTone

<!--
public enum TextRewriteTone
-->

## -description

Specifies the tone to use when rewriting text.

## -enum-fields

### -field Default: 0

Equivalent to `General`.

### -field General: 1

General purpose, prioritizing neutrality.

### -field Casual: 2

Friendly, conversational with a priority on casual language.

### -field Concise: 3

Clarity and brevity are prioritized for easy understanding.

### -field Formal: 4

Professional, business-like and avoids bias, slang, or overly casual language.
