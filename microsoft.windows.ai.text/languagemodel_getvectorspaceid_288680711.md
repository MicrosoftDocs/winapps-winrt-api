# Microsoft.Windows.AI.Text.LanguageModel.GetVectorSpaceId

<!--
public Guid GetVectorSpaceId ();
-->

## -description

Returns the unique identifier of an [embedding vector](../microsoft.windows.ai.foundation/embeddingvector.md) object.

## -returns

The unique identifier of an [embedding vector](../microsoft.windows.ai.foundation/embeddingvector.md) object.

## -remarks

In a vector space model, words, sentences, or entire documents (or "embeddings") are transformed into vectors, or lists of numbers that represent the semantics of the text.
