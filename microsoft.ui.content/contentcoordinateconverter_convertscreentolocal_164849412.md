# Microsoft.UI.Content.ContentCoordinateConverter.ConvertScreenToLocal(Windows.Graphics.RectInt32)

<!--
public Windows.Foundation.Rect ConvertScreenToLocal (Windows.Graphics.RectInt32 screenRect);
-->

## -description

Converts the screen coordinates of the supplied [rect](/uwp/api/windows.graphics.rectint32) to local coordinate space (adjusted for [RasterizationScale](/windows/windows-app-sdk/api/winrt/microsoft.ui.xaml.xamlroot.rasterizationscale)).

## -parameters

### -param screenRect

The [rect](/uwp/api/windows.graphics.rectint32) to convert from screen coordinates to local coordinate space (adjusted for [RasterizationScale](/windows/windows-app-sdk/api/winrt/microsoft.ui.xaml.xamlroot.rasterizationscale)).

## -returns

The converted [rect](/uwp/api/windows.foundation.rect).
