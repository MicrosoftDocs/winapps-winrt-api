# Microsoft.Windows.BadgeNotifications.BadgeNotificationGlyph

<!--
public enum BadgeNotificationGlyph
-->

## -description

Defines constants that specify which glyph is shown on the badge notification.

## -enum-fields

### -field None: 0

No glyph.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-0-none.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with no notification badge.":::

### -field Activity: 1

Activity glyph. Shows an accent colored circle with two curved arrows.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-1-activity.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with an accent colored circle that has two curved arrows in it.":::

### -field Alarm: 2

Alarm glyph. Shows an accent colored circle with a bell.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-2-alarm.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with an accent colored circle that has a bell in it.":::

### -field Alert: 3

Alert glyph. Shows a red circle with an asterisk.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-3-alert.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a red circle that has an asterisk in it.":::

### -field Attention: 4

Attention glyph. Shows a red circle with an exclamation mark.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-4-attention.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a red circle that has an exclamation mark in it.":::

### -field Available: 5

Available glyph. Shows a green circle.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-5-available.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a green circle.":::

### -field Away: 6

Away glyph. Shows a yellow circle.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-6-away.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a yellow circle.":::

### -field Busy: 7

Busy glyph. Shows a red circle.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-7-busy.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a red circle.":::

### -field Error: 8

Error glyph. Shows a red circle with an X.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-8-error.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a red circle that has an X in it.":::

### -field NewMessage: 9

New message glyph. Shows an accent colored circle with an envelope.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-9-newmessage.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with an accent colored circle that has an envelope in it.":::

### -field Paused: 10

Paused glyph. Shows an accent colored circle with two vertical lines.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-10-paused.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with an accent colored circle that has two vertical lines in it.":::

### -field Playing: 11

Playing glyph. Shows an accent colored circle with a right facing triangle.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-11-playing.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with an accent colored circle that has a right facing triangle in it.":::

### -field Unavailable: 12

Unavailable glyph. Shows a gray circle.

:::image type="content" source="images/badge-glyph-12-unavailable.png" alt-text="A taskbar icon with a gray circle.":::

## -remarks

Values of this enumeration are passed to the [BadgeNotificationManager.SetBadgeAsGlyph](badgenotificationmanager_setbadgeasglyph_1070366469.md) method.
